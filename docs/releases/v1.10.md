Everything between the first public release and this one: the journal stays out of the keyboard's way, Stash teaches itself on the first launch, and it updates itself from here.

## The journal

- Opens next to the text cursor of the app you are typing in, like Win+V — under the caret, or above it when there is no room below. `Open at the Cursor` in the menu turns that off.
- Never takes the keyboard: whatever you type goes to the app underneath, and a click anywhere outside closes the journal.
- ↑ and ↓ move the selection, Return pastes, Esc closes — as hotkeys, so the app you are typing in never loses focus. `Intercept Keys` leaves those keys to your app instead.
- Paste and copy are separate: the orange button pastes where your cursor is, the one beside it only puts the clip on the pasteboard. `Close After Selection` closes the journal afterwards.
- An image opens in Preview from its thumbnail, and a clip marked as the current one is easy to spot.
- Themes and interface language moved into the menu: System, English, Русский, and Stash Auto as the default look.

## Accessibility access

- Stash pastes by simulating ⌘V, which macOS will not allow without Accessibility access. A screen of its own now asks for it and opens the right settings page.

## Getting started

- A five-slide tour plays on the first launch and takes no keys at all: click the left third to go back, the rest to go on. `How to Use Stash?` plays it again.

## Updating

- Stash checks GitHub ten seconds after it has settled and once a day after that. A new version lights an orange dot on the menu bar icon; nothing ever pops up over what you are typing.
- The update screen shows what is new and installs it with one button: it downloads the image, checks its signature, replaces the app and relaunches it.
- Releases are signed with a certificate that stays the same from build to build, so macOS keeps the Accessibility permission across updates instead of treating every build as a new app.

## About

- `About Stash` names the version and the studio behind it, with the wordmark set in Booker Display.

## Windows

- Updates, About and the tour each open in a window of their own instead of covering the journal.
- The update and About windows hang under the menu bar icon and are only as tall as their content needs.
- The tour opens in the middle of the screen and closes into nothing: the journal is not what was asked for.

**Updating from 0.9:** the signature changed with this release, so Accessibility access has to be granted once more. Remove the old Stash entry from System Settings → Privacy & Security → Accessibility with − and let Stash ask again.

**Installing by hand:** the app is not notarized, so macOS blocks the first launch. Click Done, then open System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down and click Open Anyway next to Stash.
