Text and links are told apart in the list.

## The journal

- A text clip is marked `Aa` and a clip that is one web address — with or without its `https://` — is marked with a link, instead of the clip's first letter. A mail address counts as text: it is pasted as a word, not opened.

## The front page

- The project's page on GitHub now shows the journal at work: the animations are assembled from the journal's own views, so they look exactly like the app, in the light and the dark look.

**Installing by hand:** the app is not notarized, so macOS blocks the first launch. Click Done, then open System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down and click Open Anyway next to Stash.
